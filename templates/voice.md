# voice.md (template)

Every skill in this kit reads this file. Fill it in once, in your own words,
and copy it to `~/.claude/linkedin/voice.md`. Skip it and everything comes out
sounding like everyone else.

The fastest way to fill it: paste three of your own posts into Claude and say
"write my voice.md from these". Then edit by hand.

> Keep your filled copy private. It stays at `~/.claude/linkedin/voice.md` and
> is gitignored, so it never ends up in a public repo.
